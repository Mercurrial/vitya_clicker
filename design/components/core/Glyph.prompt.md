Minimalist stroked geometric glyph for KARDASHEV generators and upgrades — never skeuomorphic icons.

```jsx
<Glyph name="fusion" size={22} tint />
```

Names: generators — `photovoltaic`, `geothermal`, `fusion`, `antimatter`, `dyson`, `neutron`, `blackhole`, `galactic`; upgrades — `overclock`, `coherence`, `cascade`, `zeropoint`, `catalyst`, `horizon`. Pass `tint` to render in plasma cyan when the entity is active/unlocked, otherwise it renders in mid grey. Typically wrapped in a rounded `GlyphTile`.
